# Issue #64 — Stage 2B generalization protocol (Phase 0 only)

**Status: `OUTCOME_FREE_DRAFT_REQUIRES_RESEARCHER_APPROVAL`.** This is a design artifact, not a frozen execution authorization or an experimental result. No fresh cohort has been generated, no test truth or estimate was read, and no PostgreSQL held-out query was measured.

The protocol in [`protocol-v1.json`](protocol-v1.json) is an independent, versioned follow-up. It does not modify `protocol-v4.json`, any v1–v4 evidence, the Stage 2B runner, `ess-postgresql`, `ess-advisor`, or the paper. Historical formal v4 is explicitly not treated as valid new held-out evidence because its coefficient defect was documented; corrected-log PR #58 artifacts remain validation-only until reviewed and pinned.

## AreCEL correspondence and adaptation

Wang et al. define single-table conjunctive cardinality queries and discuss equality, open-range and closed-range predicates (Section 2.1). Their workload generator varies predicate count, predicate columns, in-/out-of-domain centers and range widths (Section 4.1); their motivation includes the absence of a common workload generator and the sensitivity of workload results (Section 2.5). Section 3 defines symmetric natural Q-error as `max(estimate, truth) / min(estimate, truth)` on a positive domain. Table 4 reports P50, P95, P99 and Max Q-error for Census, Forest, Power and DMV.

This study borrows the metric family and workload-shape reporting, not AreCEL's estimator training, experimental numbers, or claims. The proposed primary `Q1=max(E,A,1)/max(min(E,A),1)` is a one-row-floor adaptation, explicitly not the paper's unmodified metric. Natural Q-error is also reported on the positive-positive subset with its denominator, while zero agreement, estimate-only zero, truth-only zero, invalid and nonfinite cases remain explicit. Q1 is measured from PostgreSQL's declared plan estimate; the model's positive pre-clamp log loss is reported separately and is not interchangeable with rounded/clamped `Plan Rows`.

P95 is primary per dataset; P50/P99/Max are secondary. Each policy's per-query Q1 across the two sample replicates is aggregated by mean log Q1 (geometric mean) and then summarized by nearest-rank P95. The paired estimand is instead computed consistently as the within-query mean of `log(Q1_PESS)-log(Q1_Nominal)` over the same two matched replicates, at Decimal precision 80 and rounded half-even to 50 decimal places; the recorded delta sign defines benefit/harm/tie. Arithmetic-Q1 paired effects are sensitivity only. A missing estimate or truth makes that query `UNRESOLVED`; any unresolved query makes the primary cohort result `INCONCLUSIVE`, with valid-subset numbers descriptive only. A missing OID trace does not remove an otherwise valid Q1 observation; it makes physical-use attribution unverified. Plans and sample replicates are not independent queries. No pooled primary metric is proposed, and subtracting policy P95 values is not treated as a paired-delta quantile.

## Workload, independence and physical boundary

The Issue #59/PR #60 v4 audit is descriptive, outcome-free structural evidence. It reports exact/template overlap across the legacy splits and strict exact-two-predicate eligibility of only 0.52%–4.16% across the four datasets. Thus a generalized evaluation should allow 1..D predicates and retain all valid generated queries; graph conflict, usable MCV payload and selected-OID trace are strata, not silent eligibility filters. Existing test sets are not fresh: prior exposure bars their reuse for the primary prospective evaluation.

The protocol specifies a deterministic manifest recipe rather than claiming that a cohort already exists: per dataset, fixed seeds 6401–6404, a SHA-256 counter PRNG, Issue #59's exact/template fingerprint byte conventions, 1,000 calibration and exactly 1,000 held-out template groups, one query per group, and pre-outcome predicate/operator/graph-stratum quotas. Candidate templates are generated and filtered by schema/structure only; literals come from deterministic source-table row/domain draws. `cohort_recipe.py` implements fingerprint/split/PRNG primitives. `schema_capacity.py` and `build_schema_capacity_report.py` enumerate schema-only combinatorial capacities, exact d=1/d=2 split assignments, and attainable size quotas. None reads source rows, outcomes, or creates a cohort. A later authorized manifest builder must implement the full recipe and hash the complete manifest before opening any held-out truth, estimate or trace.

The fingerprint gate covers all four legacy AreCEL workload splits and the exposed Stage 1A #38 and Stage 2B #48 cohorts. Issue #59 published overlap aggregates but not raw digest sets; those hashes must be regenerated from workload SQL/schema only, never `truth.json`. Any missing coverage or collision blocks cohort freeze. The 1,000-group target is exactly 1,000 measured queries because the group/query ratio is fixed at 1:1; it is not evidence that those groups are available. Each split requires at least 500 queries with >=3 predicates. Operator/graph strata are quota-eligible only when their pinned schema-only upper bound is >=100; each then requires >=100 distinct candidates in each split after historical quarantine, otherwise freeze is `BLOCKED`. The fixed attempt-pool result is checked before any outcome access; a pool shortfall does not redefine schema feasibility.

### Schema-capacity correction to the cohort quotas

The second review correctly found that equal predicate-bin allocation was impossible: the frozen template fingerprint removes literals, so a column contributes only its supported operator variants. The pinned schemas provide 60/38/16/42 total single-predicate templates for Forest10/Census13/DMV11/Power7, respectively—far below the former 200–250 per-split target. Exact fingerprint hashing assigns these across calibration/held-out as 31/29, 14/24, 5/11 and 19/23. The d=2 spaces are also exactly enumerated and split-hashed (global totals 1,620/628/105/756); this avoids spending the same finite templates in both splits. The categorical equality restriction is source-derived from AreCEL's pinned `wsf_uniform`/`wsf_exponential`; the proposed generator explicitly extends its numeric operator set with strict `<` and `>`.

The frozen schema-derived per-split allocations are:

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Forest10 | calibration | 31 | 200 | 257 | 256 | 256 | 769 |
| Forest10 | held-out | 29 | 200 | 257 | 257 | 257 | 771 |
| Census13 | calibration | 14 | 200 | 262 | 262 | 262 | 786 |
| Census13 | held-out | 24 | 200 | 259 | 259 | 258 | 776 |

All bins sum to 1,000 per split and the combined calibration+held-out target in each bin is no greater than its exact global schema capacity. `schema-capacity-report.json` records the complete nonzero predicate-bin × operator-family × exclusive-graph-class upper-bound table; zero cells are explicitly omitted/zero. The code and tests check marginal conservation and exact low-bin split capacities. Notable structural limits: DMV11 has only one numeric column, so its total upper bounds are one closed-range-only template and four one-sided-inequality templates; the conflicting-edge class with exactly one maximal matching has capacity zero for all four simple candidate graphs. These classes are explicitly structural-infeasibility findings, not post-outcome exclusions. All other schema-feasible strata still face the independent one-million-attempt, split-pool and historical-quarantine gates; no actual cohort or literal feasibility is claimed.

Calibration, coefficient derivation and resource optimization happen separately on each dataset. The four bounded candidate universes are inherited from the outcome-free Issue #59 audit (six Forest pairs, six Census pairs, ten DMV adjacent pairs, six Power adjacent pairs); they are not exhaustive across table columns. Budgets 0/2/3/5 use one additive candidate-count proxy unit and do not assert physical maintenance additivity. Every optimal resource set is recorded; a deterministic lexical rule selects one deployed set, while PG creation order is separately fixed to candidate order.

Keep four layers distinct:

1. **Model objective:** corrected-log Nominal and task-wise PESS objectives, selected independently per dataset from validation only.
2. **Resource decision:** unordered extstats set at each budget; report all minimizers/ties and chosen deployed set.
3. **Physical PG behavior:** actual catalog object definitions/OIDs, usable MCV payload and per-query selected-OID/clause traces; `EXPLAIN` alone is insufficient.
4. **Held-out evaluation:** PG row estimate versus cardinality truth under paired query IDs; no result feeds back into model choice, resources or cohort membership.

PostgreSQL’s matching correspondence is scoped and conditional. Queries with >2 predicates and conflicting candidate edges are retained, but a matching in the abstract graph is not asserted to be what PostgreSQL selected. Mismatch and incomplete traces are reported. Calibration uses only sample replicate 0, with Empty and every candidate singleton measured once per calibration query; sample replicate 1 is never used for selection and is reserved for evaluation sensitivity. A matched physical block requires byte-identical dataset/table/schema/build/GUC/target/sample-seed metadata and an equal SHA-256 of the sampled stable-row-ID set across the relevant treatment clones; otherwise paired primary comparison is blocked. Treatment build order is a frozen seeded permutation, with DDL and one `ANALYZE` sequence recorded per isolated clone. Query-error validity is separate from mechanism validity: absent OID trace leaves an otherwise valid Q1 in the primary cohort but blocks a claim about which object PostgreSQL used. Missing/invalid estimate or truth is `UNRESOLVED`, and any such row makes the full-cohort primary result `INCONCLUSIVE` rather than silently shrinking its denominator.

## Phase 0 feasibility and stopping point

Source-derived from merged #59: four datasets and candidate definitions are documented; structural overlap exists; DMV11/Power7 physical payload/traces are `NOT MEASURED`; no fresh cohort is available from this protocol. Historical Forest/Census PR #58 evidence is not repurposed as new held-out evidence. Corrected-log validation artifacts require PR #58 source/input pin review. Sample identity, runtime and disk caps need a real preflight. All these are explicit `NOT RUN`/approval gates.

The hard upper cap is 100,000 PostgreSQL estimate-plan attempts across calibration and held-out phases: calibration is at most 4 datasets × 1,000 queries × 11 sets (Empty + ten candidate singletons) × one sample replicate = 44,000; held-out is at most 4 × 1,000 queries × seven deduplicated treatment sets × two sample replicates = 56,000. These caps include failed plan attempts; count-query plans are never retried, though cluster setup may be retried once before any plan. No extra measured pilot or replacement query is permitted. The proposal also sets 24 hours per dataset and 100 GiB disposable data, subject to researcher feasibility approval. At least two datasets must pass all gates for cross-dataset replication language. Stop per dataset on integrity, trace, comparability or resource-cap failures; preserve the failed state and do not substitute queries or rerun based on outcomes.
